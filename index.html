Big programs and smaller firms.

Formal internship applications are only half the search. Smaller firms can be excellent targets for direct outreach, especially for hands-on design and manufacturing work.

Smaller firms: if no internship is posted, send a short email and your resume directly. Many do not run a formal campus recruiting cycle.
Larger companies: use the official careers portal and apply as soon as a relevant 2027 posting opens.

Automatic updates

The online version can refresh itself.

The deployable version includes an automatic job collector and scheduled GitHub workflow. When connected to a job-search API, it searches the Greenville, Spartanburg and Anderson areas, categorizes relevant engineering internships, removes duplicates and refreshes the site.
